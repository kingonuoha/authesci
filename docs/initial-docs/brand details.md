1. Basic Company Info
What does your company do (in one simple sentence)?
Authesci is a collaborative research and job platform that connects scientists, research collaborators, and employers within a trusted ecosystem.
What problem do you solve for your customers?
We make it easier for scientists to find collaborators, access research opportunities, and get hired, while helping employers find qualified talent quickly and reliably.

Community and networking features

2. Target Audience
Who is your primary customer?
Scientists, researchers, graduate students, labs, research institutions, biotech companies, and employers looking for scientific talent.
What’s the main pain or frustration they face that you help fix?
Scientists face major challenges such as difficulty finding skilled collaborators, limited access to research opportunities, and job platforms that aren’t tailored to science. Employers also struggle to find verified scientific talent, while early-career researchers remain largely invisible. These gaps slow collaboration, discovery, and career growth.
Any common objections people might have before choosing your service?

“Are the job listings legitimate?”
“Is my research information safe?”
“Is this platform active enough to be worth joining?”

3. Value & Trust
What makes your company different from competitors?
Authesci stands out by focusing exclusively on science and research roles, creating a platform tailored to the unique needs of scientists, researchers, and institutions. At its core, Authesci is built on trust, every profile is verified to ensure authenticity, professionalism, and credibility within the ecosystem. Unlike traditional job platforms, Authesci is intentionally designed for collaboration, enabling researchers to connect, form partnerships, and work on meaningful scientific projects together, not just search for jobs.
The platform also makes project posting seamless for labs, research teams, and independent investigators, allowing them to find the right talent or collaborators with ease. Opportunities on Authesci are curated to support early- and mid-career scientists who often struggle with visibility and access in the scientific world. Most importantly, Authesci is developed with both the African scientific landscape and the global research community in mind, bridging gaps, amplifying talent, and fostering a truly inclusive space for scientific growth and discovery.
